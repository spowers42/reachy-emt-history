# Glossary

**Trainee**: The person practicing history taking. Either a student working toward a certification or a currently licensed provider keeping skills sharp.

**Provider Level**: The Trainee's certification stage, one of: EMT Student, EMT, AEMT Student, AEMT, Paramedic Student, Paramedic. Shapes what is expected of the Trainee and how feedback is pitched.

**Environment**: The care setting a Case takes place in, one of: Urban/Suburban, Rural, Wilderness. Shapes which emergencies are plausible and the circumstances around them. Our Wilderness means remote, delayed-care settings; it is _not_ the NEMSIS/USDA geographic "Wilderness" code.

**Case**: A single simulated emergency-medicine patient, randomly generated when a Session starts and hidden from the Trainee. Holds the Underlying Condition, Chief Complaint, and every answer the Patient can give. The Case is the source of truth for both the Patient's answers and the Debrief.

**Underlying Condition**: The actual medical problem behind a Case (e.g. sepsis, stroke, AMI, hypoglycemia, severe dehydration). Always a real diagnosis, never a vague complaint. It is what the Trainee's Field Impression is judged against.

**Chief Complaint**: What the Patient (or Caller) says is wrong, in their own words. It is often vague ("I just feel weak"), and many Underlying Conditions can share one. _Avoid_ treating a Chief Complaint such as weakness or malaise as a Case's condition.

**Caller**: The person who requested EMS: the Patient, or a bystander, friend, or family member. What the Caller reported becomes the dispatch information the Trainee starts with, which may differ from what the Patient says.

**Patient Insight**: How well the Patient understands their own Underlying Condition. It varies by Case: a long-term diabetic may know exactly what is happening, while an elderly Patient with multiple medical problems may not. These are tendencies, not rules.

**Session**: One practice run: a Case is generated, the Trainee interviews the Patient, states a Field Impression, and receives a Debrief.

**Patient**: The role Reachy plays during the interview, answering in character only from the Case.

**Instructor**: The role Reachy switches to once the interview is over, delivering the Debrief.

**Debrief**: The Instructor's feedback at the end of a Session, judged against the Case and the Trainee's Provider Level.

**SAMPLE**: History mnemonic: Signs/Symptoms, Allergies, Medications, Pertinent past history, Last oral intake, Events leading up.

**OPQRST**: Complaint-characterization mnemonic: Onset, Provocation/Palliation, Quality, Region/Radiation, Severity, Time.

**Field Impression**: The Trainee's working diagnosis, formed from the history gathered and stated at the end of the interview.

**Differential**: The set of alternative conditions a Trainee considers alongside the Field Impression. Expected depth grows with Provider Level, following NREMT expectations.

**Wilderness Condition**: A condition specific to the Wilderness Environment and outside normal EMS, e.g. Acute Mountain Sickness, HAPE, HACE. _Avoid_ the bare abbreviation "AMS", which in EMS usually means Altered Mental Status; spell it out.

**Vitals**: Measurements (pulse, BP, respirations, SpO2, etc.) the Trainee may request; supplied as Case data, not physically performed.
